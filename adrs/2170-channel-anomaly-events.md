# ADR 2170: Per-item and cache anomaly events for the MCP overseer

**Status:** Accepted
**Issue:** #2170
**Builds on:** ADR-1966-b (channel events), ADR 2124 (episode model, poll-overdue verdict), ADR 2125 (label actor attribution), ADR 2114 (non-blocking slot acquire), ADR 2096 (cooldowns consumed on admission), ADR 1831 (retry backoff)

## Context

In the 2026-10-11 in-situ session every anomaly that mattered was found only in `.fabrik/fabrik.log`: retries killed by `max_wall_time` that then waited 30+ minutes for a worker slot (A3/A8), a feedback gate holding #2125's advance for over an hour with nothing dispatched (A11, engine defect #2160), and a light reconcile reverting recent engine label writes, producing phantom label pushes (A9). `internal/attention` cannot see the second: an active cooldown classifies as `waiting`, and a hold that re-stamps `feedback-pending` every poll never becomes `stalled`.

## Decision

1. **Five item-scoped events.** `retry-starved` / `retry-resumed` and `item-wedged` / `item-unwedged` (announcement and recovery, all immediate) and `cache-divergence` (a point event, digest-eligible, no recovery). All follow the normal repo/issue/milestone scoping.
2. **Observation only.** `engine/channel_anomaly.go` reads the Store through `Peek`, the poll verdict through the health mirror and slot counts through `len`/`cap` of the semaphore. Facts the Store lacks arrive through void, nil-safe, panic-recovered hooks in `channel_hooks.go` (`emitRetryScheduled`, `emitDispatchDeferred`, `noteGateHold`, `noteGateClear`) called from the engine files that already know them. The R5 source scan and the observation-only sim are unchanged.
3. **Episodes are in memory only** and every dedup key carries the episode's anchor time and a per-process sequence, as for `pipeline-stalled`: the hub persists dedup keys, so a static key would swallow every later episode.
4. **Clock domain.** Due times and hold starts are stamped with `e.now()` at the hook. `LastAttemptAt` is wall-clock and `CooldownAt` entries are consumed on admission, so neither can supply them; `e.now()` is also what lets the sim cross a threshold with `Clock.Advance`. The tick and debounce stay real-time.
5. **retry-starved scope.** Stage retries after a non-completing run (kills, max-turns, resume failure, tools-denied, generic error, clean-but-incomplete) and the usage-limit / api_error did-not-run exits. Not covered: reinvoke and comment-review dispatches and cooldown holds (`dep-blocked`, `feedback-pending`, `review-blocked`); a hold that never reaches a dispatch is `item-wedged`'s business. An episode opens when `now - max(due, last usage-limit lift) >= threshold` with no worker entered since. Reason: `no worker slot` (a deferral that keeps recurring - recorded no earlier than the due time and within the lapse window of the check - or the general pool full; carries `slots_in_use`/`slots_max`), else `poll overdue`, else `unknown` - reported rather than silenced because "due, free slot, healthy poll, not running" is itself anomalous. Paused, blocked, awaiting-input and awaiting-done items, items that left the stage, and items held by the account-wide usage-limit hold are not reported. An announced episode always closes with `retry-resumed`; its `how` meta is `dispatched` or, when the item stopped waiting without a dispatch, `closed`, `advanced`, `held` or `gone`.
6. **Kill cause is a typed error.** `claudeerr.KilledError{Cause, Err}` (`wall-time-kill`, `inactivity-kill`) wraps, at a single site in `runClaude`, the error of a run the engine killed on a timeout that did not complete. It is transparent (`Unwrap`), so every `errors.As` classification and the `Attempts`/`max_retries` accounting are unchanged; a run that emitted its completion marker before the kill is left alone. `tests/sim/simclaude` gets `WallTimeKilled`, because the sim replaces the whole invoker and `runClaude`'s timeout path never runs there; `engine/claude_killed_test.go` covers the real wrap.
7. **item-wedged scope.** Gate sites call `noteGateHold` on every evaluation that holds. The feedback gate's next step is always the engine's own (dispatch a reinvoke), so it uses the configured threshold directly. The review, CI and merge gates wait on people and machines and already end in their own timeout pauses, so for them the threshold is `max(configured, backstop + 2 × poll)` (3 × review wait timeout; the CI backstop), and a normal CI wait never fires. A hold ends - `item-unwedged` - when a worker enters the item, the column changes, the gate clears (feedback only) or the hold stops being re-confirmed for `max(3 × poll, 2 min)` - extended by the recheck interval (10 × poll) for the feedback, review and CI gates, which are re-evaluated that rarely once nothing bumps the item (the feedback gate records a `feedback-pending` cooldown of that length, the review gate `review-blocked`), so their hold can still age to its threshold. The merge gate re-checks every half poll and needs no extension. While the poll is overdue nothing is opened or lapsed (`pipeline-stalled` explains it). An item with a worker, a deferred dispatch or a pause is not wedged.
8. **cache-divergence.** `itemstate.Change` gains `Path` (`reconcile`, `deep-fetch`, `probe`), `StatusFrom`/`StatusTo` and `EngineStatusWrite`; `Origin` could not distinguish these paths. `onChange` keeps a mutex-guarded ledger of recent engine label and column writes; a change on a refresh path that contradicts an in-window entry publishes one event (`what`, `written`, `observed`, `path`, `age_seconds`) and consumes the entry, and every event built from that change (the label events, the label-derived `merged` / `blocker-cleared` / `landing-verification-failed`, `column-changed` and `closed`) carries `unverified=true`. A webhook change to the same key clears the entry. A column revert may be a legitimate external move that only the reconcile saw; the event text says so. `channelevents.MetaUnverified` is the single definition, so #2169's provenance work reuses it and widens the rule to other unattributable deltas.
9. **Defaults and configuration.** `--retry-starved-threshold` 5 min, `--item-wedged-threshold` 10 min, `--cache-divergence-window` 5 min (env `FABRIK_RETRY_STARVED_THRESHOLD`, `FABRIK_ITEM_WEDGED_THRESHOLD`, `FABRIK_CACHE_DIVERGENCE_WINDOW`), read once at startup and classified in `internal/workerenv`. The first two are floored at 2 × the poll interval so raising the poll interval cannot make them fire early (ADR 1831). Unlike #2124's fixed `pipelineOverdueFactor`, R4 requires these to be configurable.
10. **Catch-up** includes open `retry-starved` and `item-wedged` episodes (current state); `cache-divergence` has none.
11. **Neutralisation seams.** `SetAnomalyDetectorsDisabledForTest` (one flag per detector) shows each acceptance test is not vacuous. `SetReviewReinvokeDisabledForTest` suppresses the review-feedback reinvoke dispatch, reproducing the observable shape of #2160 (a hold with nothing dispatched) independently of that defect, so the item-wedged sim test keeps reproducing whether or not #2160 is fixed.

## Consequences

- Detection latency is the threshold plus at most the 30 s deriver tick.
- A restarted daemon that is still in a condition raises a fresh episode.
- `item-wedged` cannot tell a hold the engine will clear itself from one it never will; the thresholds, the backstop margin for external gates and the worker/deferral exclusion keep that to the cases where the engine has done nothing for a long time.
- The divergence ledger is cleared only by a webhook change or an overwrite. If a reconcile agrees with an engine write, and a human then removes the label and that removal reaches the cache only through a later reconcile inside the window, it is flagged as a revert. That is acceptable for an `unverified` hint (known limitation).
- A missed or doubled `cache-divergence` is possible when an engine write and a reconcile apply within the same instant; the entry is consumed per divergence to bound duplicates.
